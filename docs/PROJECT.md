## How the mini project is graded

From the CS2881R Grading Scheme. The mini project is **20%** of the course grade.

| Component | Weight | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| Paper reproduction | 30% | Reproduce the main result of the chosen paper. |
| Extensions | 30% | Conduct **1-2 variations** on the original experiment. |
| Writeup and analysis | 30% | Explain the methodology, interpret successes and failures, and discuss what the experiments establish and what remains uncertain. |
| Documentation / presentation | 10% | Organized code, clear instructions for reproducibility, and worklogs. Presented in a clear way. |

Course-wide notes from the same document:
- "Although we allow and encourage use of generative AI, students should demonstrate
  understanding of the methods they use and the choices they make. Work that is 'AI slop' will
  receive grade deductions. We would prefer a project with negative results led by humans
  compared to more 'impressive' results entirely generated by AI."
- "Informative negative results can earn full credit. … An unsuccessful run alone is not the
  same as a well-supported negative result."
- "Group members should contribute equally. We ask for detailed group contributions in each
  writeup."
- Staff may move a grade up or down a letter for qualitative factors, including creative
  approaches, collaboration, AI slop and extraordinary participation.

## Presentation

- **Template deck:** [ASIMOV-2.0 Deck Template](https://claude.ai/artifact/X4zHzrwjBHkvDCbv7uTRMj),
  19 slides. It is a cover, five numbered section dividers (Background, Prior work,
  Reproduction, Hypotheses, Results), title-only content slides, paper-vs-ours frames for
  Figs. 4–6, an H1/H2 slide, accuracy-vs-cost chart frames and a closing slide. Export to
  PowerPoint from the page's Share menu.
- **Theme ("drafting table"):** warm paper `#F2F0EA`, ink `#16181A`, safety orange
  (`#E2581E` hatching, `#B4430F` text, `#FF7A3D` on dark). Archivo for titles, IBM Plex Mono for
  labels; install both fonts before opening the .pptx. Orange hatching means a robot keep-out
  zone and appears only on the cover and section dividers. Corner brackets mean bounding boxes
  and only frame figures and images.
- **Conventions:** write each results title as the finding in one sentence. Use the small label
  above a title only for a figure or hypothesis ID. There are no page numbers.
- **Outline:** [`docs/presentation_outline.md`](presentation_outline.md). Each owner writes
  their own findings; nothing there is pre-written.
