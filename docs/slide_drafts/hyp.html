03 · HYPOTHESES

Two hypotheses

H1

Fast, low-cost safety detection

Decision models can identify safety risks at lower cost and within the latency that robotics and physical AI require

H2

Performance by injury type

Model accuracy varies by injury category, revealing differences in how well models handle different types of physical-safety inference

H1 (tentative, Tyler): classifier models are better positioned to identify safety risks within the latency robotics and physical AI require. H2 (tentative, Tyler): changing the prompt, and possibly some fine-tuning, should give small models basic accuracy gains while keeping latency roughly within the time needed to intervene. Training data must come from outside the eval set.
