H2 result on the text benchmark, 319 items. Each decision model is shown at its best setting from a grid of up to 8 (wording x question mode). I picked the best cells after seeing all 319 items, so they are exploratory until the cross-validated analysis confirms them. No decision model matched GPT-5 (75.2%), but Jev at its best setting reached 73.2% for $0.038 per 319 items (about $0.12 per 1,000) vs $1.24 for GPT-5 ($3.90 per 1,000), and 0.70 s vs 3.29 s per item. Decision models fall furthest behind on Q4 (severity after the action). Free tier and self-hosted models (hollow dots) have no per-call price, so they sit in their own column, and their GPU time is not in the dollar figure. On routing, sending the lowest-confidence 75% of Jev's items to GPT-5 gives a mean of 72.3% at $2.98 per 1,000 vs $3.90 for GPT-5. A perfect router would reach 79.2%, so confidence is an imperfect signal (results/part2_designs/tables.md). Latency is the median per item, and cumulative rows add 4 calls.
