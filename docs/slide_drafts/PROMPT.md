## Rules of the deck format

Every slide is one `<section>` on a fixed 1920x1080 canvas, all styles inline from a closed subset: px lengths,
hex colors, no classes, `<style>`, `margin`, `z-index`, `em` or `var()`. Text 24px or larger (SVG chart labels
may be 22). Layout comes from flex or grid with `gap` and `padding` (the sections use
`padding:112px 128px 176px`). Elements: `h1-h3`, `p`, `ul/ol`, `div`, `img`, `table`, `svg` (52 KB max, needs
`aria-label`). Speaker notes go in one `<aside>` as the last child. Palette: ink #16181A, background #F2F0EA,
orange #E2581E and #B4430F, rules #C9C3B6. Fonts: Archivo and IBM Plex Mono.
